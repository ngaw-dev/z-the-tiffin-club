FAQ Accordion

Fraunces question headers, rotating chevron, ember highlight border on expand.

Why is there only one dish?

Each morning the kitchen commits to a single dish and cooks it in a limited batch. It keeps the food exceptional and the decision effortless — no menu, no options, no negotiations.

When does ordering close?

Delivery orders close at 4:00 PM. Pickup orders stay open until 8:00 PM, when the pickup window (6–8 PM) ends. Same-day orders only — no pre-ordering at launch.

How do I pay?

Pay on handover — cash or card machine at the door for delivery, or at the counter for pickup. There's no online payment at launch.
